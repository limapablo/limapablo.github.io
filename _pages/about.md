<h1>Business context, analytical depth and practical execution.</h1>
<p class="page-lead">I am a Data & Analytics professional based in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. My background combines business administration, applied analytics and technical implementation.</p>

<section class="content-section">
<h2>How I work</h2>
<p>I hold a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration from UFRRJ and an MBA in Data Science & Analytics from USP/ESALQ. That combination strongly influences how I approach data work: I do not see analytics as an isolated technical discipline, but as a way to understand operations, improve decisions, reduce manual work and create measurable value.</p>
<div class="principles-grid">
<div><span>01</span><h3>Business context first</h3><p>A technically sophisticated solution is not useful if it answers the wrong question.</p></div>
<div><span>02</span><h3>Reproducibility matters</h3><p>Good analytics should not depend on hidden manual steps or knowledge held by one person.</p></div>
<div><span>03</span><h3>Communication is part of the solution</h3><p>Dashboards, reports and models create value only when people understand what they mean and what action they support.</p></div>
</div>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
<h2>Professional focus</h2>
<p>My current work and portfolio are centered on operational analytics, business intelligence, Python and SQL workflows, process automation, analytics engineering concepts and applied machine learning.</p>
<p>I particularly enjoy projects that require ownership across the analytical lifecycle: understanding the problem, preparing the data, defining metrics, building the analytical model and communicating the result clearly.</p>
</section>
