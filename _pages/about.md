# About me

I am a **Data & Analytics professional** based in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, with a background that combines business, analytics and applied technology.

I hold a **Bachelor's degree in Business Administration from UFRRJ** and an **MBA in Data Science & Analytics from USP/ESALQ**. That combination strongly influences how I work: I do not see data as an isolated technical discipline. I see it as a way to understand operations, improve decisions, automate repetitive work and create measurable business value.

## Professional focus

My main areas of interest are:

- Data Analytics and Business Intelligence
- Python and SQL for analytical workflows
- Power BI and semantic modeling
- Analytics Engineering
- Process automation
- Applied Machine Learning
- Operational and process analytics

I particularly enjoy projects that require ownership across the full analytical lifecycle — understanding the problem, preparing the data, defining metrics, building the analytical model and communicating the result clearly.

## How I approach data work

I value three things in analytical projects:

**Business context first.** A technically sophisticated model is not especially useful if it answers the wrong question.

**Reproducibility matters.** Good analytics should not depend on manual steps that only one person understands.

**Communication is part of the solution.** Dashboards, reports and models only create value when people can understand what they mean and what action they support.

## Research

My MBA capstone at USP/ESALQ applied **K-Means clustering and geospatial analysis** to sanitation and education indicators across Brazilian municipalities.

The project later evolved into a scientific article associated with SIMEP 2025.

**DOI:** [10.29327/23189258.1068040](https://doi.org/10.29327/23189258.1068040)

## Current direction

I am currently expanding my portfolio toward more complete **end-to-end analytics projects**, combining Python, SQL, relational databases, Power BI and automation.

My goal is to build the depth required to work on increasingly complex data problems while keeping a strong connection to business outcomes.
