## Introduction

In this project, I am applying **Machine Learning** techniques to analyze data related to **sanitation** and **education** in Brazil. The main objective is to understand the distribution and relationship between these two factors and their implications for public policies, through the **K-Means Clustering** algorithm.

## Project Objective

The project aims to perform **clustering of Brazilian municipalities** based on **sanitation** and **education** indicators. The goal is to understand how different municipalities group based on these variables and how this can influence governmental decisions and social interventions.

## Methodology

To carry out the analysis, I used the **K-Means algorithm**, one of the most popular unsupervised learning methods for data analysis. It helps identify patterns in data without requiring predefined labels. The clustering process helped divide municipalities into similar groups based on sanitation and education variables.

The project code was developed in **Python**, using libraries like **Pandas** for data manipulation, **Scikit-learn** for K-Means implementation, and **Matplotlib** for visualizations.
